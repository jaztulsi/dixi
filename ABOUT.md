# Dixi

**The web, hands-free.**

Dixi is a web browser you talk to. You say what you want in your own words, and Dixi does it on the real website, the same way you would with a mouse and keyboard. It opens the site, searches, picks the right result, plays the video, adds the item to your cart, and reads you the answer out loud.

It also listens while you're still talking. Each part of what you say starts running the moment you've finished saying it, so by the end of your sentence Dixi is usually already partway through.

> "Dixi, play lofi beats on YouTube."
>
> Dixi opens YouTube, searches for lofi beats and starts the first video, all from one sentence.

---

## The idea

Most of what we do on the web takes many small steps: find the site, find the search box, type, scroll, pick a result, find the right button, choose a color, choose a size, open the cart. Each step is easy, but together they take time and attention, and they all need your hands and your eyes.

Dixi turns that around. You say the goal, and Dixi takes care of the steps. You don't need to know where a button is, what a site calls its menu, or even the site's exact address. You just say what you want, the way you'd ask a friend sitting at the computer for you.

## The name

*Dixi* is Latin for **"I have spoken."** That's the whole experience: you speak, and it's done.

The look follows the name. Dixi's design is white marble and clear glass, with bold black type, and its mascot is a small marble figure with a laurel wreath. It sits on Dixi's start page and in the live caption, reacting as Dixi listens and works.

## What Dixi can do

### Open any site

Say the name of a site and Dixi takes you there: "open YouTube", "go to Apple Music", "open Amazon". You don't need the exact address. Dixi works out which site you mean, even one it has never opened before, and it remembers the sites you use, so getting back to them next time is even quicker.

### Search anywhere

Search on the site you name, the way you'd type it yourself: "search for wireless earbuds on Amazon", "search for coffee on Apple Maps", "search for Severance on Apple TV". Dixi finds the site's own search box, types your search and opens the results.

### Shop

Dixi handles shopping from the first search to the checkout page:

- **Find a product:** "buy me an iPhone 18 and AirPods".
- **Pick from results:** "add the first one to my cart", "open the third one".
- **Choose options:** color, size and storage, said naturally: "an iPhone 18 Pro Max in silver with 512 gigabytes".
- **Manage your cart:** "open my bag", "remove the AirPods", "make it two", "one more of these".
- **Check out:** "check out" takes you straight to the store's checkout, ready for you to sign in and confirm your order.

Dixi checks its work against the store itself. When it adds something or changes a quantity, it confirms the change with the store's own cart before it tells you it's done.

### Watch and listen

Dixi controls video and music with the words you'd naturally use:

- **Play and pause:** "play", "pause it", "resume".
- **Volume:** "turn it up", "quieter", "mute", "unmute".
- **Speed:** "faster", "slow it down".
- **Moving around:** "rewind", "skip ahead", "start over", "next video", "skip the ad".
- **Viewing:** "captions on", "full screen".

You can say several at once: "pause it and turn it up."

### Get around

- **The page:** "scroll down", "go to the top", "go to the bottom".
- **Pages:** "go back", "go forward", "refresh".
- **Tabs:** "open a new tab", "next tab", "previous tab", "close this tab".
- **Zoom:** "zoom in", "zoom out", "normal zoom".
- **Any button by name:** "click sign in", or the name of whatever you see on the page.

### Ask questions

Ask Dixi about the page you're on, and it reads the answer straight off the page and says it out loud:

- "What's the total in my bag?"
- "How much is the iPhone by itself?"
- "How many subscribers does this channel have?"
- "What is this video about?"
- "What's in my cart and how much is it?"

Every answer about a page shows the exact line it came from, and Dixi checks that line is really on the page before it answers, so what you hear is what the page says.

You can also ask about anything else, like "what's the newest iPhone?" or "how does this compare to the last one?". Dixi looks it up on the web and answers in a sentence or two.

### Bigger tasks

For open-ended tasks, Dixi's **AI Agent** works through a site step by step, reading the page, clicking and typing like a person would until the task is done. You can open the Agent panel to watch each step as it happens and see the result at the end.

## Talking to Dixi

**Start with "Dixi."** Dixi listens all the time but only acts when you start with its name, so the TV, music or a conversation in the room won't set it off. Right after it acts, you can keep going for a few seconds without saying its name again: "Dixi, open YouTube" … "search lofi beats" … "play the first one". If you'd rather not use the name at all, you can turn the wake word off in Settings.

**Say it your way.** There are no set phrases to learn. "Get me the iPhone 18 and some AirPods" and "buy an iPhone 18 and AirPods" mean the same thing to Dixi. You can add "please", "um" or "can you", and say the details in any order.

**Chain it.** Say a whole sequence in one breath: "open YouTube, search lofi beats, play the first video, turn it up." Dixi runs each step in the order you said it, starting each one as soon as you've finished saying it.

**Change your mind.** Say "stop", "cancel" or "never mind", or press **Esc**, and Dixi stops what it's doing straight away.

**Or type.** The bar at the bottom of the window takes typed commands and website names too, for times when talking isn't convenient.

**A quick question when it matters.** If what you said could mean more than one thing, like "I want an iPad" when there are several iPads, Dixi asks which one you meant and carries on with your answer.

## What you see

**The start page.** A clean marble page with the mascot, the words "Just say what you want." and a few example commands to try.

**The live caption.** A small black bar at the top of the window, like a Dynamic Island, that shows:

- the words Dixi is hearing, as you say them;
- the steps it's taking, each ticking off with a ✓ as it's done;
- answers to your questions, with the line on the page they came from.

The caption never gets in the way of clicking the page.

**The browser.** Below the caption is a full, modern browser with tabs, built on the same engine as Chrome, so sites look and work the way you're used to. You can still click and scroll yourself at any time, and "Open in Browser" sends the current page to your usual browser.

**Its voice.** Dixi answers out loud in a calm, natural voice. Prices, sizes and numbers are spoken the way a person would say them: "one thousand one hundred ninety-nine dollars", "two hundred fifty-six gigabytes", "fifteen point eight million subscribers".

---

## How it works

**1. Hears.** Your voice is turned into text in real time, word by word, as you speak. Dixi watches for its name and for where one instruction ends and the next begins.

**2. Understands.** Dixi works out what you mean, whatever words you use and in whatever order you say them. Everyday steps go to a very fast AI model, and shopping and harder requests go to a stronger one, so it's quick when it can be and careful when it needs to be.

**3. Acts.** Dixi reads the page the way the page is built, with its real buttons, links, menus and search boxes, and uses them like you would. That's why it works on sites it has never seen before.

**4. Checks.** After each step Dixi confirms it worked: the page changed, the video is playing, the cart has the item. Then it moves on to the next step.

**5. Answers.** When you ask something, Dixi finds the answer on the page or on the web and says it out loud.

---

## Built for speed

- **It acts mid-sentence.** Dixi doesn't wait for you to stop talking. Each instruction runs as soon as it's complete.
- **It looks ahead.** While you're still speaking, Dixi starts preparing for what you're likely to ask and warms up the connection to the site you're heading to.
- **It gets quicker with use.** Once Dixi has understood how you ask for something on a site, it remembers, and the next time is quicker still.
- **It answers quickly.** Spoken replies start about half a second after the answer is ready.

---

## Privacy

Dixi has no account to create and no Dixi servers. It runs on your computer and talks directly to the services it uses. Your voice goes to **ElevenLabs**, which turns it into text and gives Dixi its voice. What you say and what's on the page you're viewing go to **Cerebras** and **Anthropic's Claude** so Dixi can understand and act. You can pause the microphone at any time.

---

## Who it's for

- **People whose hands are busy:** cooking, working, holding a baby, eating lunch at the desk.
- **People who find a mouse and keyboard slow or hard to use,** for whom saying it is simply easier than clicking through it.
- **Anyone who'd rather say "add the first one to my cart"** than click through five pages to do it.

## Where Dixi is going

Dixi is currently in beta. Here's where we're taking it next:

- **Draw on your screen.** Ask Dixi to sketch a diagram, circle the button you're looking for, or draw arrows between ideas, right on top of whatever is on your screen, while you talk it through.
- **See you, not just hear you.** With your permission, the camera lets Dixi notice where you're looking and what you're pointing at, so "open that one" just works. It can also tell when you've stepped away.
- **Take on bigger goals.** Go from single steps to whole tasks: "plan a weekend trip under $500 and show me the options side by side." Dixi will handle the nuance ("the cheaper one, unless it arrives after Friday") and check in with you only at the moments that matter.
- **Learn the way you work.** Dixi will remember your favorite sites, sizes and stores, so over time you say less and it does more.
