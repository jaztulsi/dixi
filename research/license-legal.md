# License + legal

> Collected by the Dixi planning council (dixi-99), 2026-10-29, from the other reports in this folder and the plan's §10 and §6d. Read-only research.

## Summary (≤300 words)

**Nothing from any studied repo is copied into Dixi. Ideas are rewritten and credited in `ATTRIBUTIONS.md`.** That rule settles most of the license risk:

- **No license** (all rights reserved, so inspiration only): VisionOS (its npm `"ISC"` field is a default, not a grant), echonav (its pyproject says MIT but there's no LICENSE file), BrowserForBlind, treehacks-amogus, AI-Restaurant-Reservation-Agent, booking-agent, SusBench, and three of the small token tools.
- **Copyleft. Copying code would force Dixi under the same license:** Holmes and dinner-reservagent are **GPL-3.0**. Accessible-AI-Agent, Skyvern, workflow-use and Lightpanda are **AGPL-3.0**, and BrowserOS is **AGPL-3.0 with a CLA**.
- **Not open source:** cordum (BUSL-1.1); RTK is partly BUSL per its badge.
- **Permissive** (MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD-2), so ideas can be cited, and code could be reused with attribution if ever needed: pipecat, livekit/agents, Handy, screen-voice-agent, VoxStride, browser-use, Stagehand, Agent-S, restaurant-cli, travel-agent, Progent, PIC, buried-injections, OpenAdapt, rrweb, magentic-ui and agent-browser.

**Platform terms (read 2026-10-29):**
- OpenTable's terms (§13) bar AI assistants, and §18.2 treats an agent's actions as the user's.
- Resy has deactivated accounts that used agents.
- Yelp's and Google's reservation APIs are partner-only.
- Tock's and Amazon's consumer terms are UNVERIFIED.

So Dixi hands off on OpenTable and Resy, and books live only where the terms allow.

**Legal context:**
- Amazon blocked Meta Muse on 2026-09-20.
- In Amazon v. Perplexity, the Ninth Circuit vacated the injunction on 2026-08-04; the rehearing denial is medium confidence.
- The FTC fined accessiBe $1M for overclaiming, so Dixi never says it "makes sites accessible".
- ADA Title II deadlines for libraries are 2027-04-26 and 2028-04-26, so a pilot needs our own VPAT.
